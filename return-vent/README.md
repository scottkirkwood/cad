# Modern Minimalist Perimeter-Slot Return Vent (Fittes / Aria Style)

A parametric, 3D-printable replacement for builder-grade stamped steel return vents, inspired by the **Fittes LITE / LITE+** architectural air vents.

Designed for the **Bambu Lab H2C** (330 mm × 320 mm bed) in [fittes_return_vent.scad](fittes_return_vent.scad).

---

## How It Stays Securely on the Wall (Cannot Fall Out)

1. **Subframe Wall Retention**:
   - The subframe has an outer 18 mm flange that overlaps the drywall (impossible to fall into the duct).
   - An 18 mm deep rear collar drops inside the drywall opening for positive alignment.
   - It screws directly into the wall studs / drywall anchors using:
     - Countersunk screw holes hidden inside the shadow trench, AND/OR
     - Outer flange screw holes matching your original vent's exact side screw positions.
2. **Center Faceplate Retention**:
   - **Anti-Drop Gravity Shelf Lugs**: The subframe features mechanical bottom resting ledges. The center plate's weight physically rests on these ledges, so gravity literally cannot pull or slide it down.
   - **6 Neodymium Disc Magnets**: 6 magnetic points (3 top, 3 bottom) pull the plate firmly back against the wall so it cannot tilt forward.
   - **Positive Indexing Bosses**: Raised 7 mm registration nubs nest into matching recessed sockets on the rear of the center plate, preventing any lateral or vertical shifting.
   - To remove: Simply reach your fingers into the top air slot and pull forward to break the magnetic hold.

---

## Sized for the Bambu H2C (Single-Piece Seamless Center Plate)

- **Center Faceplate**: **310 mm × 170 mm** — **100% ONE SINGLE PIECE**.
  - Prints flat on your Bambu H2C build plate (330 × 320 mm) with zero split lines or seams across the front face!
- **Wall Mounting Subframe**: **390 mm × 240 mm**.
  - Splits into Left and Right halves (195 mm × 240 mm each) with alignment dowel pin sockets.
  - Screws permanently to the wall and stays hidden behind the floating faceplate.

## Recommended Print Settings

- **Material**: **PETG** or **ABS/ASA** (matte white recommended).
- **Bed Surface**: **Textured PEI plate** — slice with the front faces **face-down** on the plate for a subtle matte texture that matches painted drywall.
- **Walls & Infill**: 4 wall perimeters, 20% Gyroid infill. No supports needed.

---

## Hardware Required

- **Magnets**: 6× or 12× Neodymium disc magnets (8 mm dia × 3 mm thick, or 10×3 mm).
- **Mounting Screws**: 2–4× Standard drywall screws (can reuse your existing wall holes).
- **Frame Seam Pins**: 4× 3.8 mm dowel pins (or short segments of 1.75 mm filament) for frame alignment.
